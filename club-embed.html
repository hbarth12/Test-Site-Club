The club

Damascus has no shortage of conferences and workshops. But it lacks places for people across institutions, professions, generations, and communities to routinely encounter one another regularly.

Every Thursday, we gather in the leafy district of Mazra'a in central Damascus for food, refreshments, and conversation. Throughout the month, we host debates, panels, book talks, screenings, and other events on Syria’s politics, economy, history, and culture.

The lending library

The club houses a growing collection of Syrian history, politics, law, journalism, and literature. Anyone can borrow from it—or recommend a title, contribute a book, and help curate a shelf.

About our community

Our members include journalists, researchers, writers, public officials, diplomats, civil-society leaders, artists, and students.

With membership comes a regular place to meet, talk, read, introduce people, propose ideas, and return the following week.
