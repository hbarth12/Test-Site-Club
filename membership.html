A dependable place in the city

Thursday Club brings together journalists, researchers, writers, public officials, diplomats, civil-society leaders, artists, and students.

Members come from different institutions, professions, generations, communities, and political traditions. The club asks only for curiosity about Syria and a willingness to engage seriously with people beyond one’s usual circles.
