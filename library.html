The lending library

Browse the shelves.

The club houses a growing collection of Syrian history, politics, law, journalism, and literature. Anyone can borrow from it—or recommend a title, contribute a book, and help curate a shelf.

Browse by subject

The online catalog is being assembled. In the meantime, these are the main areas of the collection. Visit on Thursday evening or contact the club to ask whether a particular title is available.

Syrian history

Social, political, local, and oral histories.

Politics & public life

Government, political economy, institutions, and public policy.

Law

Constitutional, legislative, administrative, and comparative law.

Journalism

Reporting, media history, ethics, and practical craft.

Literature

Fiction, poetry, memoir, essays, and translation.

Reports & periodicals

Research publications and material that can be difficult to find elsewhere.

A searchable title-by-title catalog will be added as the collection is inventoried.

Borrowing is simple.

Find a book, leave your name and contact details with the club, and return it by the agreed date. Reference material may need to remain in the house.

You do not need to be a member to borrow. Members can also recommend acquisitions, contribute books, and propose a curated shelf or reading group.
